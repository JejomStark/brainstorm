# Content profile

An editorial need: article, video, training, documentation, presentation.

## Branches of the tree

1. **Audience**: who reads or watches, what they already know, what they look for.
2. **Promise**: what the audience will be able to do or think at the end, in one sentence.
3. **Angle**: what sets this content apart from what already exists.
4. **Material**: sources, examples, figures available (the attachments first).
5. **Format**: medium, length, tone, platform constraints.
6. **Structure**: the main parts, the hook, the call to action.
7. **Production**: who does what, deadline, review.

## Default deliverables

`brief` recommended (production brief, for a person or an agent); `plan` for a series or a production in steps; `other` for a synopsis or a detailed outline.
